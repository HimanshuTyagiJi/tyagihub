❓ Frequently Asked Questions on Digital Tools

Which domain registrar works best with your DNS setup guides?

Any standard ICANN-accredited registrar works seamlessly—including GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains (Squarespace), Hostinger, and Cloudflare Registrar. All our DNS guides provide universal record syntax (A, CNAME, MX, TXT) compatible with every DNS manager.

Why choose Zoho Mail Free over simply forwarding custom domain emails to Gmail?

Standard email forwarding to personal Gmail often fails SPF/DMARC authentication, causing your outbound replies to land in spam folders or show suspicious "via" headers. Zoho Mail Free provides official MX/SPF/DKIM cryptographically signed delivery, ensuring 100% inbox deliverability and professional brand credibility.

What are the limits of hosting a website on GitHub Pages?

GitHub Pages provides 100GB/month bandwidth, a 1GB repository size limit, and automatic Let's Encrypt SSL certificates via Fastly CDN. It is ideal for static websites, HTML/CSS/JS applications, and documentation, though it does not execute server-side code like PHP or Node.js.

How does Cloudflare Turnstile protect web forms without annoying image puzzles?

Unlike legacy reCAPTCHA puzzles that force users to click traffic lights and fire hydrants, Cloudflare Turnstile runs lightweight cryptographic telemetry non-intrusively in the browser. Real human visitors pass silently in milliseconds with zero friction.

Is Zoho Invoice really 100% free with no monthly limits?

Yes. Zoho made Zoho Invoice 100% free forever for all businesses worldwide. You can create unlimited client profiles, generate unlimited GST-compliant tax invoices, track estimates, and receive UPI payments with zero subscription fees.
