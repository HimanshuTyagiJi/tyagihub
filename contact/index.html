Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a reply?

We review incoming correspondence daily and typically reply within 24 to 48 business hours. If you are reporting a configuration or DNS error, attaching relevant screenshots or specifying your domain provider helps us assist you faster.

Do you provide one-on-one consulting?

Our primary goal is publishing free, open-access tutorials for everyone. If you have an interesting cloud setup question or challenge that could benefit our broader community, email us at contact@tyagihub.in and we will work on turning it into an open step-by-step guide.

Do you accept sponsored reviews or guest posts?
