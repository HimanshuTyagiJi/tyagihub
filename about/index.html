Himanshu is an independent software developer, Android application builder, and technology researcher based in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. He founded TyagiHub to cut through online noise and provide clear, verified, step-by-step documentation on digital tools, cloud software, and productivity systems.

Our Story & Founder Journey

Starting out as an independent software and Android developer in India, I constantly ran into the same frustrating roadblock: modern business tools, cloud setups, and software APIs are surrounded by expensive paid subscription paywalls or surface-level AI-generated summaries that skip critical configuration steps.

Whether it was configuring DNS zone records for custom domain email, finding genuine zero-cost billing software that handles Indian GST compliance, or setting up lightweight relational project trackers without enterprise software budgets, the practical answers simply were not available in one trustworthy place.

TyagiHub was created to fill that void. Based in Ghaziabad (Uttar Pradesh), our mission is to physically test, document, and publish actionable, jargon-free tutorials that empower solopreneurs, freelance developers, creators, and small business owners to operate professional digital systems without paying unnecessary recurring fees.

What We Cover on TyagiHub

  • Digital Tools: In-depth walkthroughs on setting up lifetime free custom domain business email (Zoho Mail), generating 100% free GST-compliant invoices (Zoho Invoice), embedding spam-protected website forms (Zoho Forms), and configuring cloud utilities.
  • Productivity & Workflow Systems: Designing clean relational databases in Airtable to manage clients and deliverables, setting up minimal solopreneur dashboards in Notion, and optimizing day-to-day digital task management.
  • Zero-Cost Architecture: Helping emerging founders and indie developers maximize official free-forever tiers from global SaaS providers, saving thousands of rupees in recurring overhead.

Our Editorial Standards & E-E-A-T Commitment

At TyagiHub, we hold ourselves to rigorous publication and testing standards:

  1. 100% Hands-On Verification: We never publish theoretical advice or rephrased documentation. Every DNS record, DKIM key, tax calculation formula, and database relation is built and tested directly in live production environments.
  2. Original Visual Guides: Every tutorial includes custom, high-resolution visual architecture diagrams and clear screenshots so readers can verify their setups at each milestone.
  3. Strict Editorial Independence: TyagiHub is 100% independently funded and operated. We do not accept sponsored posts or paid software endorsements. If a free tier has limitations (such as mobile IMAP restrictions on Zoho Mail Free), we transparently disclose them and provide genuine workarounds.
  4. Real Community Engagement: We personally review reader inquiries and technical questions sent to our inbox, updating tutorials whenever software providers modify their interfaces.
