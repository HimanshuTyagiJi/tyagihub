About TyagiHub

Independent technology publication • Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India

Himanshu Tyagi

Founder, Software & Android Developer • Ghaziabad, India

Himanshu is an independent software developer, Android application builder, and technology researcher based in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. He founded TyagiHub to cut through online noise and provide clear, verified, step-by-step documentation on digital tools, cloud software, and productivity systems.

Our Story & Founder Journey

Starting out as an independent software and Android developer in India, I constantly ran into the same frustrating roadblock: modern business tools, cloud setups, and software APIs are often surrounded by expensive enterprise subscriptions or incomplete tutorials that skip critical configuration steps.

Whether it was configuring DNS zone records for custom domain email, finding genuine free billing software that handles Indian GST compliance, or setting up lightweight relational project trackers without large software budgets, the practical answers simply were not available in one trustworthy place.

TyagiHub was created to fill that void. Based in Ghaziabad (Uttar Pradesh), our mission is to test, document, and publish clear, step-by-step guides that empower solopreneurs, freelance developers, creators, and small business owners to operate professional digital systems efficiently.

What We Cover on TyagiHub

  • Digital Tools: In-depth walkthroughs on setting up custom domain business email (Zoho Mail), generating GST-compliant invoices (Zoho Invoice), embedding contact forms (Zoho Forms), and configuring cloud utilities.
  • Productivity & Workflow Systems: Designing clean relational databases in Airtable to manage clients and deliverables, setting up solopreneur dashboards in Notion, and optimizing day-to-day task management.
  • Practical Tech Blueprints: Helping founders and indie creators maximize official free tiers from software providers, saving significant recurring overhead.

Editorial Guidelines & Standards

At TyagiHub, we hold ourselves to rigorous publication and testing standards:

  1. Practical Verification: Every DNS record, tax calculation formula, and database relation is verified directly in live environments before publication.
  2. Original Visual Guides: Every tutorial includes custom visual architecture diagrams and clear step-by-step illustrations so readers can follow along easily.
  3. Strict Editorial Independence: TyagiHub is 100% independently operated. We do not accept sponsored posts or paid software endorsements. If a free tier has limitations, we transparently explain them and provide practical alternatives.
  4. Community Feedback: We actively review reader questions sent to our inbox, updating tutorials whenever software providers modify their dashboards or APIs.
